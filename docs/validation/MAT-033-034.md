## Completed scope and acceptance

MAT-033 supplies four association/identity questions and four independent
claim-relative evidence questions. The fixed association alternatives remain
`same_matter / related / different / insufficient`; topic, matter and occurrence
are distinct propositions. Same wording across projects, tasks, executions and
people cannot itself authorize identity or attachment. Ambiguous aliases stay
unresolved. The actual provenance service keeps a derived summary and its source
in one dependence group.

The evidence fixtures distinguish planned funding from an established allocation.
They bind explicit claim components, and support, contradiction and qualification
can coexist. Fixture answers test contracts and exact replay; they are not
independent semantic accuracy labels. Altered question meanings, wrong bindings
and malformed siblings refuse positive partial projection. No result changes an
association, source record or accepted identity.

MAT-034 supplies four cause-specific change questions and three separate recovery,
objective and novelty questions. The actual lifecycle episode holds an unrelated
success, applies matching recovery, and then holds a late report of an older
happening rather than reopening recovery. Resolved and still relevant remain
separate dimensions. The actual attention service changes update to digest under
an explicit host preference with unchanged source facts; uncertainty stays quiet
without a human question, and repeated observed information stays quiet with
`already_delivered`. Queued receipts do not advance delivered knowledge.

The source-correction episode uses a real assessment, queued intent and capture
transport. Its existing `source_index_ref` dependency changes after an intake
correction; dispatch refuses with `E_DEPENDENCY_STALE`, zero sends and no delivered
baseline. A host tracking current source meaning must declare that selector or
publish a corrected assessment. Historical observation pins alone do not
subscribe to future corrections. This belongs in MAT-046's integration contract.

A public comparison, claim/evidence question and protected comparison each run
through the actual `ExecutionReceiptStore`, committed resource budget and, for
the protected case, `AccessService.dispatch`. All three attempts are primitive
valid and remain non-consequential; durable replay retains the original answers.
The separate association receipt test proves exact reuse with one executor call.
Protected rendering and inspection refuse access after read permission revocation.

## Reference boundary

The bounded comparison transform retains exact assessment, condition, result,
purpose/audience, preference/responsibility and delivery snapshots. It uses the
assessed matter's scope, including claims about a domain value or another entity.
Missing optional context is not proof of absence. Future, foreign, wrongly typed
or oversized context refuses preparation without mutating the original packet.

Preparation 1.1 freezes nested references to metadata committed at the original
cut and includes their actual read dependencies. Bare mutable references use the
existing historical revision reads, bounded to 128 candidates. A test takes its
cut from an actual assessment commit receipt, revises matter metadata later, then
rebuilds byte-identical packet and comparison audits without the later revision.
Protected current-egress views retain their existing currentness refusal.

Knowing an attribution identity does not make its source content available. A
known claim attributed to an observation of unknown availability remains
inspectable with incomplete coverage, an explicit `unknown_availability` omission
and no source text. Reference checks are memoized; only the comparison subtree is
transformed, avoiding repeated reads of the original mechanical comparison pairs.
Original records remain available through their pins. The preparation fingerprint
changes explicitly; this adds no storage, client or execution infrastructure.
