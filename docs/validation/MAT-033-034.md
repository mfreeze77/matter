## Scope and acceptance evidence

The definitions reuse MAT-027's registry and renderer, MAT-028 packets,
MAT-026/029 execution and MAT-030 receipts. They add no provider or action path.
The small comparison transform binds exact host contexts and their versions,
controls and availability to the original packet cut; it preserves explicit
assessment field omissions and refuses budget overflow.

`tests/semantic/test_evidence_families.py` covers the fixed association choice,
separate topic/matter/occurrence dimensions, ambiguous aliases, derived-summary
dependence, independent support/contradiction/qualification/event-established
heads, altered question meanings and malformed sibling suppression. Its actual
SQLite/budget/receipt test records one fixture invocation, replays exact retained
answers, then reuses the original attempt without a second invocation. Its
protected rendering/projection test uses the existing access service and refuses
inspection after source read permission is revoked.

`tests/semantic/test_attention_families.py` covers all four explicit causes,
original condition binding, unavailable/future/foreign or wrongly typed contexts,
bounded packet refusal and context identity. Its actual lifecycle episode holds
an unrelated success and applies a matching recovery. A late offline replay is
submitted through the actual recurrence boundary and held; a resolved condition
can remain relevant. The actual attention
episode changes update to digest with one selected host preference and unchanged
source facts. Unknown evidence remains quiet without a human question; queued
receipts do not advance delivered knowledge, and repeated observed information
remains quiet with `already_delivered`.

The correction/send episode declares the existing `source_index_ref` as a
positive dependency of its assessment before queuing advice. A subsequent real
intake correction changes that head and the queued send is refused with
`E_DEPENDENCY_STALE`, zero transport calls and no delivered baseline. A consumer
of current source meaning must declare that selector (or process and publish a
corrected assessment); immutable historical observation pins alone do not
subscribe to future corrections. An initial probe without that declaration sent
the old advice into its synthetic capture sink. This is a host/profile integration
requirement to carry into MAT-046, not a new implicit watcher in the families.

Domain-value and non-matter entity claim subjects both work with matter-scoped
contexts. A context scoped only to the subject entity is refused, matching the
existing host attention boundary. Future snapshot receipts are refused even when
their host content was backdated.

PR18's synthetic `runtime-inputs.json` is copied byte-for-byte to
`tests/fixtures/semantic/civic_runtime_inputs.json` for four objective-binding
checks (company, advocacy, district IT and finance). This is authored input
conformance, not a StateCivics replay or observed JEV analysis. The separate
family/recovery JSON files identify their answers as agent-authored recordings,
not independent labels or a semantic accuracy evaluation.
