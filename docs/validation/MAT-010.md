## Temporal selection and exact coverage

`matter.time` separates earliest availability from declared applicability.
Selection requires explicit pinned packets and known cuts. It partitions
included, excluded and unknown references, preserves input order, and never
guesses an effective interval from another time field. Unknown endpoints are
not infinity. Fractional precision is compared exactly through nanoseconds;
equal timestamps do not order events. A reversed known interval refuses;
equal closed endpoints describe one instant and an open equal bound is empty.

Each supported observation intake adds its immutable pin to the exact scoped
source-namespace catalog. Corrections remain distinct admitted records.
Duplicate delivery and exact successful replay do not add another admission.
A new catalog has no historical baseline. An authorized host must explicitly
declare and inventory legacy observations before it can support absence.
Initialization merges that inventory with already captured observations and
can happen only once. The host owns the declaration's completeness.

Coverage binds the exact query, sources, observed interval, external catalog
and snapshot descriptors, completeness or failure reason, original knowledge
cut, adapter, authority, mode and prior revision. Admission history reconstructs
the exact earlier catalog population and initialization state using guarded
current reads. Later arrivals or later initialization cannot rewrite an old
proof. Complete replacement still needs fully known, nonempty elapsed coverage
and no matching or indeterminate evidence to become `adequate_empty`.

Typed predicates AND supported exact scalar clauses or whole canonical JSON
extension values. Missing optional metadata is indeterminate. Canonical type
identity is retained: Boolean `true` cannot substitute for integer `1`.
The implementation does not parse source text or infer source independence,
supersession, claim authority or business meaning.

## Protected replacement and direct negative dependencies

Replacement acts on this coverage family's explicit host-derived membership.
An admitted retirement capability plus adequate complete replacement may retire
an omitted machine-owned member. Operator-owned membership remains active;
reviewed membership retains its original pin and requests review; rejected
membership remains a tombstone. Changed reviewed pins cannot silently replace
reviewed evidence. Ownership cannot be demoted and rejection cannot be
resurrected. Other source/query/interval families retain their state.

Referenced host projections and source records remain immutable. This service
does not delete claims, decide that a proposition is false, or resolve matters.
Incomplete or failed empty input, disabled retirement and incremental silence
preserve prior owned state. An explicitly included machine member may reactivate
on a later authorized publication.

Negative registrations bind a stable watch ID, current assessment or host
projection pin, exact coverage proof, original knowledge boundary and expiry
or next-check time. Assessments must retain the exact positive coverage pin
and canonical negative manifest. Registration checks the current catalogs as
well as the frozen proof, preventing an arrival between publication and first
registration from producing usable absence.

The original knowledge cut does not exclude later arrivals from reconsideration.
A new potentially relevant record, including one declaring later availability,
invalidates current dependent use atomically while retaining the historical
proof. The result carries `negative_scope_arrival` and exact before/after pins.
Unknown relevant occurrence or metadata causes an indeterminate invalidation.
Duplicate intake and old successful retries do not repeat the signal.

Current-use helpers take an explicit clock and perform no writes. Inclusive
expiry and review boundaries change read status without creating observations,
upgrading failed coverage or scheduling work. Changed target/coverage revisions
make the registration stale. An unrelated catalog admission alone does not.
Refreshing use requires a newly prepared authorized registration; an existing
watch ID cannot be reassigned to another derivative.

## Independent integrity and transactional evidence

Independent review reproduced and corrected concrete failures before the final
regression and package build:

- Frozen coverage must match its selected catalog's complete observation
  population and historical initialization state; a stored projection cannot
  omit evidence or relabel unattested history as complete.
- A publication receipt must actually write the selected coverage revision,
  admit its declared authority and read or write every selected catalog snapshot.
  An older unused catalog or another command's receipt cannot certify absence.
- Operation proofs require the reserved storage namespace, actual operation
  stage and system origin, self-bound creation receipt and command-digest ID.
  Copying an operation receipt into an ordinary namespace cannot confer its
  committing authority.
- Historical catalog reconstruction reproduces the exact stored projection
  bytes and digests. Equivalent digest-form and revision-form read pins remain
  usable after later admissions instead of requiring a storage-history bypass.
- Canonical negative-manifest and predicate equality distinguishes Boolean and
  integer values. Malformed watched projections never become an empty watch set;
  real backend and revision failures retain their original error codes.

Both first-arrival races are exercised. A newly occupied catalog or changed
catalog revision invalidates a previously prepared registration/replacement.
A first registration committed after ingestion preparation cannot escape that
ingestion's guarded watch discovery. Two independent replacement processes
yield exactly one current-revision winner.

There are **four actual spawned-process `os._exit` interruptions**: replacement
before and after commit, and new intake before and after commit while affecting
two registrations. Membership, observation, source indexes, catalog, affected
watches and their operation receipts roll back or survive as complete units.
Lost acknowledgements replay the exact saved result without rerunning writes.
Restart and new-file backup/restore preserve historical coverage, protected
membership and invalidated watches. Payload blobs retain their existing
separate immutable-store backup responsibility.

## Qualification limits

Bounds are explicit: 64 source selectors and 64 predicate clauses per scope;
4,096 observations per catalog/coverage population, members per replacement
family, watches per source bucket and packets per temporal selection. Exceeding
a bound refuses rather than silently truncating evidence.

Catalog/watch maintenance is guaranteed through the supported services. Trusted
custom storage handlers must maintain their own admission and indexing. Source
and legacy-inventory completeness remain host attestations. No global scan,
domain expectation engine or implicit correction/supersession policy is claimed.

This is direct registered negative-dependency invalidation. General transitive
invalidation, assessment execution, host control epochs, scheduling, provider
accuracy and native delivery remain later tickets. The twelve core record kinds,
wire version, canonical encoding and SQLite migration remain stable. Older
closed-schema readers need the additive commands, fields and private schemas;
no compatibility fallback is claimed.
