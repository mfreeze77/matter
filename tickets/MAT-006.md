## Purpose

Represent the happenings described by evidence without imposing one occurrence per observation. Preserve dependence groups so copied interpretations do not become independent confirmations and repeated real events do not disappear as duplicates.

## In scope

- Optional many-to-many occurrence membership
- Revisioned grouping corrections
- Distinct counts and provenance groups
