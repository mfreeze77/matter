## Purpose

Compose source-backed civic stage assessments with explicit research, monitored-action or opportunity objectives, then deliver only an actionable or meaningfully changed assessment to the correct audience. Audience preferences never alter underlying facts or authority.

## In scope

- Purpose-specific assessment rules
- Funding/purchasing/deadline consequences
- Per-audience previous-assessment comparison
- Quiet, digest, correction and concrete decision-request reasons

## Scope boundaries

- A universal civic importance or legal-confidence score
- Sending external notifications during implementation
- Treating every incomplete assessment as a question for the user

## Implementation

1. Define purpose inputs for monitored subjects, territory/service match, responsible owner, deadlines and known dispositions, with versioned field meanings and source provenance.
2. Compose stage/evidence/authority conclusions first; evaluate objective consequences separately and record the inputs responsible for each difference.
3. Compare with the last applicable assessment delivered to the same audience, including corrections and changed authority/source coverage; reused assessments retain their original evidence identity.
4. Apply named attention treatments and withholding reasons; missing information becomes a bounded evidence request or unresolved status unless a concrete human decision is required.
5. Revalidate source/profile/assessment freshness immediately before existing delivery handoff and preserve delivery/consumption/action distinctions.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Two audiences can receive different attention from identical civic evidence without divergent source facts.

**Verify:** Run researcher and service-fit audience fixtures and compare their shared factual assessment digest and separate consequence/delivery receipts.

### AC-2

Only a changed actionable predicate, correction or declared boundary creates a repeat update.

**Verify:** Replay repeated agenda references, new solicitation deadline and later cancellation; verify the notification sequence and quiet reasons.

### AC-3

Missing authority or source coverage does not become an approval claim or automatic user request.

**Verify:** Use unavailable evidence and unreviewed authority controls; inspect unresolved and permitted-next-read output.

### AC-4

A correction queued against obsolete evidence is withheld or regenerated before emission.

**Verify:** Change the relevant source/profile between selection and delivery and check the dependency/freshness receipt.
