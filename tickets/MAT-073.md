## Purpose

Bound retained observations and receipts while preserving enough provenance to explain decisions. Withdrawal, deletion and access revocation must invalidate dependent current uses without rewriting historical delivery claims or inventing resolution.

## In scope

- Retention classes, immutable reference receipts and source withdrawal.
- Compaction and archive interfaces with reconstruction guarantees.

## Scope boundaries

- Deploying or modifying a downstream project as part of this planning ticket without that implementation task.
- Treating synthetic checks as evidence of live provider or user outcomes.

## Implementation

1. Define retention rules per record class and host scope, including what may remain as a hash or tombstone.
2. Implement compaction that retains dependency and delivery provenance needed for the declared audit horizon.
3. Invalidate current conclusions when required evidence becomes unavailable and report reconstruction limits honestly.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Archived records can reconstruct the declared historical assessment within its retention contract.

**Verify:** Archive a multi-revision episode, restore permitted evidence, and compare canonical receipts and current stale flags.

### AC-2

Withdrawing evidence cannot silently preserve a qualified current conclusion that depends on it.

**Verify:** Withdraw a decisive source and assert dependent assessments and pending deliveries are reconsidered.

### AC-3

Ordinary maintenance stays bounded and does not require a person to close routine matters.

**Verify:** Run a long synthetic stream with configured retention and measure retained counts and maintenance work.
