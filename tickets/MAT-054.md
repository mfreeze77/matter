## Purpose

Evaluate whether recognition and interaction evidence improves anonymous speaker continuity and floor tracking. Separate floor holder, speaking track, meeting role and verified person; model chair changes and exceptions explicitly.

## In scope

- Anonymous track-to-floor compatibility
- Chair/member/witness/staff/public role intervals
- Recognition/response/overlap transitions
- Role-change and shared-mic negative controls

## Scope boundaries

- Using named recognition as acoustic identity verification
- Hard-wiring speaker 1 as chair
- Updating production speaker tracks, enrollments or human confirmations

## Implementation

1. Create role/floor candidate records from procedural events and supplied roster context, keeping names as separate permitted evidence views and source-backed role intervals.
2. Define hypotheses for recognition granted, floor taken, interruption, invitation to answer, backchannel and return to chair; retain contradictions between expected role and observed voice.
3. Track chair substitutes, temporary chair, reconvened body, prepared statement and shared-mic context as distinct regimes rather than a single session-wide label.
4. Run deterministic sequence and optional narrow semantic variants on fixed inputs; compare anonymous consistency and floor outcomes before assessing named-candidate benefit.
5. Export only evidence compatibility and uncertainty plus the implicated source spans to the experimental assessment.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Chair/floor/track/person identities remain different fields and no compatibility result creates an enrollment.

**Verify:** Validate outputs and instrument all writes in a synthetic run.

### AC-2

A recognized person who remains silent and a chair who yields are not forced into the next speech turn.

**Verify:** Use independently labeled exception cases and compare event/state/track hypotheses.

### AC-3

Role transitions are time-scoped and preserve uncertainty through shared microphones and overlaps.

**Verify:** Replay a substituted-chair/multi-body sequence and inspect alternative states.

### AC-4

Reported benefit is measured on the same frozen queries and includes role-conflict misses.

**Verify:** Compare variants with fixed acoustic candidates and independent floor labels; report unchanged/worse cases as well as gains.
