## Purpose

Make assessment the durable unit of computation. Record component results and context, reusing unchanged evidence judgments across audiences or clock movement only when their declared inputs remain equivalent.

## In scope

- Assessment operation and immutable projections
- Semantic cache keys and qualification-aware reuse
- Material-change cause classification

## Scope boundaries

- Audience transport
- Learned importance calibration

## Implementation

1. Construct input from consistent matter, evidence, candidate-set, profile, and context reads.
2. Compose conclusions, gaps, dimensions, and permitted proposals with exact receipts.
3. Key caches by semantic dependencies and temporal equivalence, excluding irrelevant delivery wrappers.
4. Check all revisions, negative scopes, and epoch at commit; retain stale attempts as non-current.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Stale input prevents assessment from becoming current.

**Verify:** Pause evaluation, change evidence, then attempt commit.

### AC-2

Audience-independent evidence can be reused while audience-sensitive judgment cannot.

**Verify:** Inspect invocation counts and manifests across two audiences.

### AC-3

Policy change and external evidence change have different cause labels.

**Verify:** Reassess fixed evidence under new policy, then new evidence under fixed policy.
