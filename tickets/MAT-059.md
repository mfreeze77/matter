## Purpose

Extend the existing phrase-pause-continuation observations into alignment-aware rhythm and interaction research: response latency, overlap, backchannels and floor transitions. Timing and conversational roles remain descriptive evidence with explicit confounds.

## In scope

- Existing pause/motif feature reuse
- Response and handoff timing
- Overlap/backchannel/interrupt patterns
- Mode/source/role-balanced comparisons

## Scope boundaries

- Assuming a timestamp gap is acoustic silence
- Inferring untimed intervals or fixed individual rhythm from sparse turns
- Combining correlated timing measures as independent identity votes

## Implementation

1. Reuse adjacent-word pause/motif and rate definitions, retaining timed/untimed coverage and the rule against skipping over an untimed word.
2. Define interaction episodes from procedural floor transitions and original-clock turn spans; distinguish response invitation, response onset, overlap, interruption and backchannel with source uncertainty.
3. Compute per-mode and per-context distributions with denominators and recording-group balance; separate chair/microphone delays from candidate person regularity.
4. Add alignment-quality and timing-perturbation controls, including partial word timing and chunk-boundary artifacts; no negative gap or overlap is silently converted to silence.
5. Evaluate each family and its interactions with procedure/lexical evidence on frozen cohorts and export only qualified descriptive support.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Existing missing/partial timing semantics survive the extension.

**Verify:** Run untimed and partially timed lexical fixtures; no synthetic pauses or articulation rates are created.

### AC-2

Response latency is conditioned on an evidenced interaction rather than arbitrary adjacent transcript rows.

**Verify:** Test chair invitation, unrelated new agenda item, overlapping speakers and missing turn boundaries.

### AC-3

Timing noise and role/channel confounds are included in the reported effect.

**Verify:** Run alignment jitter and chair/shared-mic strata on the same queries and report changes and omissions.

### AC-4

Multiple derived measures from one source occurrence do not become independent corroboration.

**Verify:** Inspect provenance-group weights and assessment evidence counts for several motifs from one turn.
