## Purpose

Add a bounded proposal/execution protocol for targeted evidence gathering. Uncertainty should not automatically launch broad research, provider escalation, or a question to the user.

## In scope

- Evidence gaps and candidate reads
- Host authorization and result ingestion
- Explicit stopping and repeated-gap suppression

## Scope boundaries

- Built-in retrieval connectors
- Unbounded research or external writes

## Implementation

1. Describe each gap by proposition, eligible evidence, scope, and mandatory counterevidence.
2. Nominate reads through a typed interface with host-approved sources and capabilities.
3. Reserve shared resources and ingest results as sourced observations with availability.
4. Stop precisely and resume only after meaningful new input, eligible source, or declared temporal/retry condition.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Budget exhaustion never becomes evidence_satisfied.

**Verify:** Exhaust reads before a mandatory source is obtained.

### AC-2

A nominated read cannot expand host permissions.

**Verify:** Propose an out-of-scope source and verify no execution.

### AC-3

Unchanged gaps do not create infinite retries or automatic human questions.

**Verify:** Replay the same unresolved gap across exhausted and renewed budgets.
