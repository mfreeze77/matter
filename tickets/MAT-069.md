## Purpose

Implement the first Oil matter pilot around recurring operational failure, qualified recovery and real recurrence. Count original occurrences, retain uncertain cause separately, invalidate stale hints, and decide attention from material change without weakening IO2-143 locality protections or requiring providers.

## In scope

- Scoped generic failure matter identity, episode and occurrence correspondence
- Versioned recurrence windows/thresholds/hysteresis and decisive exception paths
- Known matching success, source invalidation, correction and reopening
- Separate assessment validity, lifecycle, dispositions and delivered baseline
- Actionability/owner relevance and deterministic provider-disabled operation

## Scope boundaries

- Attributing a root cause solely from recurrence count
- Resolving by silence, source edit, acknowledgement, compaction or unrelated success
- Replacing IO2-143 diagnostic-only locality and its freshness/TTL safeguards
- Universal aggregation that delays direct controls or deterministic obligations
- Changing all watcher checks in the initial failure pilot

## Implementation

1. Implement a versioned Oil failure profile over neutral lifecycle and attention rules. Preserve command/tool/task/workdir/audience correspondence from MAT-066, make explicit when a host-approved relation permits broader identity, and record protected separation or merge decisions instead of collapsing on command text alone.
2. Deduplicate occurrence representations using original invocation/operation provenance. Count distinct genuine failures and sustained conditions separately from independent corroboration of a cause; retain raw occurrence references and late-arrival time semantics.
3. Define active, recovered/resolved and reopened transition evidence. Require a trusted matching success after the relevant failure; delayed or earlier success cannot close a later failure. Source hash change invalidates diagnostic assessment but leaves operational success unknown.
4. Port existing IO2-143 diagnostic exclusion, operation-key matching, occurrence-based expiry, source hash validation and recovery checks as retained controls. Reconcile generic and locality outputs as contributions to the same qualified episode without introducing a competing lifecycle.
5. Implement attention rules against the current valid assessment and the audience/purpose's last delivered baseline. Account for actual changed consequence, owned/underway next steps, dispositions, time limits and bounded recurrence thresholds; record why quiet/withheld and a reconsideration trigger.
6. Make timers and simple recurrence/recovery deterministic with no evaluator dependency. Support optional semantic cause/correspondence assessments from MAT-068 when configured, while unknown semantic results neither suppress direct obligations nor force human clarification.
7. Persist correction/reopening and pending-delivery invalidation atomically. A new failure after recovery may reopen with a new episode; redelivery of the original event, TTL expiry or compaction may not. Preserve earlier delivered history and explain what changed.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Duplicate capture does not advance recurrence, genuine repeats do, and operation/task/workdir boundaries prevent unrelated merging or recovery.

**Verify:** Run same-invocation duplicates, separate repeated invocations, two child tasks and multiple workdirs with identical command text; assert exact episode counts, identity decisions and recovery correspondence.

### AC-2

Matching success resolves the relevant condition and invalidates pending advice, whereas source edit, silence and unrelated success do not.

**Verify:** Execute failure→qualified success, failure→source edit, failed/no-output check, earlier success→later failure and different-command success sequences; assert distinct lifecycle, stale/unknown and dispatch eligibility states.

### AC-3

IO2-143 diagnostic locality and IO2-138 actionability retain their existing safety behavior under the generic profile.

**Verify:** Retain negative controls for search no-match, unknown workdir/status, non-diagnostic output, original occurrence TTL and changed-source hints; compare candidate outcomes to the pinned corrected Oil baseline.

### AC-4

Attention does not repeat an unchanged delivered condition, can surface a materially changed or newly decisive development, and requires no model or ordinary user closeout.

**Verify:** Replay delivered repeat→owned repair→recovery→new failure plus threshold/deadline boundary cases with providers disabled; assert material-delta reasons, immediate direct-control handling and zero closeout requests.

## Required test cases

- One invocation represented three times versus three actual failed invocations
- Task/workdir collisions and protected separation of similar operations
- Failure→success→new failure with late or out-of-order receipt arrival
- Source changed before delivery leaves recovery unknown and diagnostic advice stale
- Known nonzero status, unknown status, rg/grep no-match and non-diagnostic failures
- Threshold oscillation, delayed timer, real severity jump and direct cancellation
- Already-owned repair and repeated unchanged advice after a prior delivered baseline
- Provider disabled, compaction and process restart with preserved resolution

## Risks

- A too-broad operation correspondence will hide unresolved work; an overly narrow one will recreate duplicates.
- Reducing noise by waiting for a fixed count can miss decisive first occurrences or time-sensitive constraints.
- Expiring or pruning evidence must not erase unresolved conditions or falsely imply recovery.
- Generic lifecycle changes can regress existing IO2-143 safety unless its controls remain explicit acceptance fixtures.
