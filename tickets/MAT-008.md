## Purpose

Make candidate association an explicit decision boundary. An evaluator can propose a connection, while the neutral engine checks revisions, permissions, and protected dispositions before accepting it.

## In scope

- Candidate-set and proposal receipts
- Exact-key and evaluator-proposed associations
- Scoped acceptance, rejection, and correction

## Scope boundaries

- Provider candidate ranking
- Equivalence merges

## Implementation

1. Represent candidate IDs/revisions, alternatives, evidence, matching-rule version, and proposal status.
2. Build exact-key proposals and accept external semantic proposals without trusting their claimed authority.
3. Require a current candidate-set revision and permitted acceptance policy.
4. Retain rejection and correction history so replay cannot silently reattach a rejected association.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

No-match, ambiguous, and insufficient-evidence outcomes stay distinct.

**Verify:** Test zero candidates, two plausible candidates, and unavailable key evidence.

### AC-2

A stale candidate proposal cannot be accepted.

**Verify:** Race an identity update against proposal acceptance.

### AC-3

Relatedness and confidence do not confer merge or override authority.

**Verify:** Submit high-score proposals against protected dispositions.
