## Purpose

Implement a neutral delivery port and durable outbox. Refuse known stale advice at a declared dispatch boundary while describing external transport uncertainty honestly instead of claiming universal exactly-once sending.

## In scope

- Outbox, lease/fencing, and baseline checks
- Transport capability and idempotency contract
- Independent outcome receipts

## Scope boundaries

- Email, Slack, or agent transport adapters
- Automatic acknowledgement or causal attribution

## Implementation

1. Prepare immutable intents with context/dependency snapshots and permitted destination authority.
2. Fence dispatch with final freshness, recovery, baseline, epoch, permission, and budget checks.
3. Represent transport idempotency/query capabilities and explicit delivery_unknown after ambiguous failure.
4. Advance baseline only on the declared observed success milestone; append later outcomes independently.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Recovery or cancellation before dispatch prevents sending queued advice.

**Verify:** Block a fake transport boundary and mutate state.

### AC-2

Ambiguous send outcomes cannot fabricate success or blind duplicate-free retry.

**Verify:** Test transports with and without idempotency or receipt lookup.

### AC-3

Competing equivalent intents cannot repeat the same delivered delta.

**Verify:** Race workers and inspect fencing, reservations, and baseline receipts.
