## Purpose

Implement a neutral delivery port and durable outbox. Refuse known stale advice at a declared dispatch boundary while describing external transport uncertainty honestly instead of claiming universal exactly-once sending.

## In scope

- Outbox, lease/fencing, and baseline checks
- Transport capability and idempotency contract

## Scope boundaries

## Implementation

1. Prepare immutable intents with context/dependency snapshots and permitted destination authority.
2. Fence dispatch with final freshness, recovery, baseline, epoch, permission, and budget checks.
3. Represent transport idempotency/query capabilities and explicit delivery_unknown after ambiguous failure.
4. Advance baseline only on the declared observed success milestone; append later outcomes independently.

## Required test cases

- Withheld intents do not advance baseline.
- Audience change before dispatch invalidates intent.
- Delivered does not imply consumption, repair, or usefulness.
- Cancellation after completed send preserves actual transport history.
- Source/permission/baseline changes before authorization refuse sending under the original context; no token renewal.
- Restart after an ambiguous external acceptance retains delivery_unknown and conservatively charged capacity.
- A late or uncertain-order reconciliation cannot overwrite newer delivered knowledge.
- Missing configured transport refuses before claiming work; exact replay checks current READ authority without redispatch.
- Queue actor/authority and actual reserved/committed allocation match the immutable intent; early duplicate responses cannot disclose another actor's work.
- An explicit original-context withdrawal atomically retires only never-authorized work, releases unused capacity and fences an old worker; authorized or unknown sends cannot be withdrawn or refunded.
- Known pre-I/O refusal records system-origin definite non-delivery, distinct from actual transport uncertainty; resolved replay returns the real typed completion result.
- Repeated unknown polling cannot exhaust terminal-receipt capacity; contradictory transport status/reason pairs are refused.
- Unusable remote transport clocks retain the exact reported time with unavailable ordering rather than losing an actual success or overwriting newer delivered knowledge.
