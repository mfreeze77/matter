## Purpose

Implement a neutral delivery port and durable outbox. Refuse known stale advice at a declared dispatch boundary while describing external transport uncertainty honestly instead of claiming universal exactly-once sending.

## In scope

- Outbox, lease/fencing, and baseline checks
- Transport capability and idempotency contract
- Independent outcome receipts

## Scope boundaries

- Email, Slack, or agent transport adapters
- Automatic acknowledgement or causal attribution

## Implementation

1. Prepare immutable intents with context/dependency snapshots and permitted destination authority.
2. Fence dispatch with final freshness, recovery, baseline, epoch, permission, and budget checks.
3. Represent transport idempotency/query capabilities and explicit delivery_unknown after ambiguous failure.
4. Advance baseline only on the declared observed success milestone; append later outcomes independently.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Recovery or cancellation before dispatch prevents sending queued advice.

**Verify:** Use synchronized injected transports and actual control/source/context mutations around the documented atomic authorization point. Before that point assert zero sends; after irreversible sending preserve the observed outcome and invalidation history.

### AC-2

Ambiguous send outcomes cannot fabricate success or blind duplicate-free retry.

**Verify:** Inject timeout and process failure before/after claim, external acceptance and local receipt commit; exercise all declared idempotency/lookup combinations, stable-key scope/expiry and unavailable lookup. Unknown stays unknown without blind resend or baseline advancement.

### AC-3

Competing equivalent intents cannot repeat the same delivered delta.

**Verify:** Race separate SQLite workers over distinct equivalent intent IDs, expired leases and resumed stale workers; inspect actual transport call counts/keys, reservations and baseline receipts, including an unresolved earlier send.

- Withheld intents do not advance baseline.

- Cancellation after completed send preserves actual transport history.

- Restart after an ambiguous external acceptance retains delivery_unknown and conservatively charged capacity.
- A late or uncertain-order reconciliation cannot overwrite newer delivered knowledge.
- Missing configured transport refuses before claiming work; exact replay checks current READ authority without redispatch.
