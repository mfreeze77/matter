## Purpose

Measure whether semantic assessment preserves the intended lightweight experience using explicit workload denominators and budget enforcement. Record total overhead, waiting and repeated work; provider latency alone cannot demonstrate that the substrate is inexpensive or unobtrusive.

## In scope

- Attempt/call/token/byte/cost and CPU/wall-time accounting
- Declared profile budgets, percentiles and baseline comparison

## Scope boundaries

- Inventing a numerical low-overhead target from an unspecified note
- Reporting unknown usage as zero or free

## Implementation

1. Define counters for logical requests, actual attempts, cache hits, question/packet bytes, usage and costs with a versioned price basis or unknown reason.
2. Instrument CPU, queue waiting, provider time, blocking time and wall time; report p50/p95 only with sample counts and measurement mode.
3. Compare A and B under the same frozen workload and state explicit denominators for percentage overhead, including zero-baseline handling.
4. Enforce declared per-run, per-matter and per-investigation budgets before dispatch and at retry/follow-up boundaries.
5. Exercise bursts, duplicate events, rate-limit retries and unhelpful investigation to expose repeated-cost paths while retaining unresolved outcomes when limits stop work.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Actual retries and speculative questions appear in total cost and workload counts.

**Verify:** Use controlled retries and batched fixtures and reconcile counters with receipts.

### AC-2

Exhausted budgets prevent additional dispatch without marking the matter resolved.

**Verify:** Run a bounded request burst and assert call limits, explicit incomplete status and preserved evidence.

### AC-3

Unavailable usage or zero baseline time cannot generate a misleading zero-cost or percentage claim.

**Verify:** Test missing metadata and zero-duration baseline fixtures and inspect report nulls and reasons.
