## Purpose

Measure each semantic family on the frozen corpus and produce qualification records only when predeclared targets and support requirements hold. Treat threshold fitting, held-out checking and runtime applicability as separate steps, avoiding confidence-only admission or copied benchmark numbers.

## In scope

- Training-only threshold selection and held-out family reports
- Support counts, clustered uncertainty, abstention and certificate creation

## Scope boundaries

- Universal accuracy guarantees
- Promoting failed qualification because a threshold happens to exist

## Implementation

1. Declare targets, accepted-error costs, eligible classes/domains, minimum independent episode support, confidence measure and interval procedure before selecting thresholds.
2. Fit thresholds on the tuning split, including separate affirmative and negative proposition regions where appropriate; retain all tried configurations.
3. Evaluate the selected procedure on qualification episodes with accepted accuracy, coverage, per-class mistakes, abstention and provider-error denominators.
4. Use episode/provenance-aware uncertainty estimates and mark insufficient support explicitly, including zero-error samples too small to establish the target.
5. Issue passing certificates with exact semantic/model/preparation identities and failure reports otherwise; invalidate affected certificates on failed drift checks.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Failed or insufficient-support evaluation never issues a usable passing certificate.

**Verify:** Use high-confidence wrong, empty-acceptance and tiny zero-error datasets and inspect gate records.

### AC-2

Threshold selection cannot read final evaluation outcomes.

**Verify:** Instrument partition access and assert that fitting sees only the declared tuning cases.

### AC-3

Reports preserve accepted coverage, class errors, failure counts and independent episode support.

**Verify:** Recompute metrics from receipts and verify denominators plus the declared resampling unit.
