## Purpose

Qualify shared mechanisms through two synthetic profiles and adversarial episodes. Record executed checks, failures, limits, and unrun provider/domain gates before any consumer claims the core is ready.

## In scope

- Integrated episodes and fault injection
- Quiet sampling and missed-change review
- Machine-readable release evidence

## Scope boundaries

- Jev accuracy or live OIL usefulness claims
- Production StateCivics or DIAT acceptance

## Implementation

1. Freeze manifests, expected outcomes, storage settings, and reference-engine revision.
2. Execute duplicate, recurrence, association, correction, resolution, deadline, control, and cancellation scenarios across restart and competing workers.
3. Audit quiet cases for missed changes as well as delivered cases for repetition and stale advice.
4. Tie every requirement and acceptance gate to receipts, leaving failed and unrun gates explicit.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Two synthetic profiles pass shared invariants without application-name branches.

**Verify:** Inspect bindings and execute common episode cases.

### AC-2

Injected restart, stale read, cancellation, and ambiguous delivery produce only declared outcomes.

**Verify:** Compare fault schedules and durable receipts to allowed results.

### AC-3

Report claims match actual executed core evidence and distinguish unrun provider/domain gates.

**Verify:** Validate requirement-to-receipt coverage and run metadata.

### AC-4

Duplicate evidence and ordinary recovery require neither repeated semantic work nor human closeout.

**Verify:** Measure calls, queue events, and required human actions in fixed episodes.
