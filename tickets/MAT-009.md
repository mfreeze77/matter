## Purpose

Provide independent relation and merge operations. Make identity mistakes recoverable, preserve conflicting children, and ensure a protected separation cannot be defeated by a later indirect merge.

## In scope

- Typed matter relationships
- Guarded merge receipts and redirects
- Undo/split and invalidation hooks

## Scope boundaries

- Semantic proof of equivalence
- Deleting evidence to force a clean merge

## Implementation

1. Validate relationship type, scope, and cycle rules independently from equivalence.
2. Plan merges against the full revision set and record every move, retained conflict, and redirect.
3. Enforce protected undo/separation decisions across complete merged groups.
4. Restore partitions through explicit correction receipts and emit identity dependency changes.

### AC-1

**Verify:** Create A-related-B-related-C and retain three identities.

### AC-2

**Verify:** Merge overlapping children and inspect all retained records.

### AC-3

Correction cannot be silently undone by replay or indirect merging.

**Verify:** Run merge, undo/split, transitive remerge, and stale retry scenarios.
