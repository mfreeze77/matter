## Purpose

Package reusable profiles and rule definitions with stable meanings, dependencies and qualification references. Configuration changes should be validated before becoming effective and should invalidate only the conclusions they affect.

## In scope

- Profile namespace, version, conflict policy and applicability manifests.
- Configuration validation and deterministic effective-policy receipts.

## Scope boundaries

- Deploying or modifying a downstream project as part of this planning ticket without that implementation task.
- Treating synthetic checks as evidence of live provider or user outcomes.

## Implementation

1. Define a bundle containing rule meanings, parameters, declared dependencies, outcome rubrics and compatible executor qualifications.
2. Validate composition conflicts, unsupported predicates and cyclic dependencies before activation.
3. Record effective configuration per assessment and schedule targeted invalidation on approved host configuration changes.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

A malformed or conflicting bundle cannot partially become effective.

**Verify:** Attempt activation with missing rules, cycles and contradictory required effects and verify atomic rejection.

### AC-2

An audience timing preference does not change underlying source claims or accepted authority.

**Verify:** Compare evidence conclusions before and after a delivery-only profile change.

### AC-3

A changed question/rubric cannot inherit an inapplicable qualification solely by retaining its old ID.

**Verify:** Modify wording, options or preparation requirements and verify qualification mismatch is explicit.
