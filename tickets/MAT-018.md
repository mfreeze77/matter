## Purpose

Represent who a matter concerns and why without assigning global importance. Several audiences and objectives can share evidence while receiving different consequences and treatments.

## In scope

- Purpose/objective records and active intervals
- Audience responsibility and preferences
- Scoped dispositions and expiry

## Scope boundaries

- External recipient-directory integration
- User-facing settings product

## Implementation

1. Define independent purpose and audience revisions with effective intervals and responsibility scopes.
2. Bind assessments to the context components they consume.
3. Implement acknowledgement, deferral, rejection, and decision dispositions with authority and supersession.
4. Expose dependencies so delivery preference changes cannot reclassify unchanged facts.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

One matter supports different treatments with a shared evidence assessment.

**Verify:** Evaluate observer and responsible-actor audiences over fixed evidence.

### AC-2

A disposition applies only within its declared scope and period.

**Verify:** Test deferral expiry and an unrelated audience.

### AC-3

Context changes preserve matter identity and old assessment bindings.

**Verify:** Change active objective and inspect historical references.
