## Purpose

Provide scheduling and budget primitives for assessment, investigation, and attention. Clock changes can matter without new evidence, but idle timers must not repeatedly call semantic evaluators or flood catch-up notices.

## In scope

- Durable next-check and idempotent firing
- Atomic shared budget reservations
- Restart, catch-up, and cancellation

## Scope boundaries

## Implementation

1. Persist timer scope, rule versions, dependencies, firing identity, and cancellation epoch.
2. Implement due-condition processing with scheduled/actual time and coalesced catch-up.
3. Provide independent resource and attention reservation ledgers with expiry and release.
4. Trigger only affected temporal outputs and reuse unchanged semantics; trusted controls bypass this queue.
