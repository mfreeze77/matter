## Purpose

Keep results current without reevaluating everything. Negative and candidate watches must detect new evidence whose identity did not exist when the original assessment ran.

## In scope

- Positive and negative manifests
- Transitive invalidation and projections
- Freshness tokens for downstream work

## Scope boundaries

- Distributed event bus
- Reclassification of unrelated evidence

## Implementation

1. Index revision reads and typed query/coverage/candidate-set watches.
2. Emit changes from core operations and match affected dependencies by scope.
3. Invalidate transitively with deduplicated reasons while preserving history.
4. Expose commit/dispatch freshness tokens and fence recomputations racing further changes.
