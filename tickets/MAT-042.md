## Purpose

Evaluate the user-visible purpose of the substrate: better continuity and timely contributions with less repeated interruption. Use paired episodes and independently reviewed expected outcomes, and inspect quiet cases so an all-silent system cannot appear successful by emitting nothing.

## In scope

- Paired A/B outcome scoring and error exemplars
- Quietness sampling, missed-event review and delivery-stage denominators

## Scope boundaries

- Calling offline eligibility observed live usefulness
- Optimizing a single notification-count metric

## Implementation

1. Score association errors, unsupported claims, false recovery, stale advice, repeated interventions, important misses and late contributions against declared episode labels.
2. Report paired differences and episode-aware uncertainty together with counts and denominators; retain separate family and end-to-end results.
3. Audit a declared stratified sample of quiet outcomes plus all severe labeled misses, distinguishing intentional deferral from absent observations or failed evaluation.
4. Track eligibility, enqueue, delivery, consumption, action and verified outcome separately; mark unobserved live stages unrun rather than inferred.
5. Produce concrete disagreement and failure examples, identifying whether evidence, association, policy, qualification or timing caused the error.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

A candidate that suppresses every update fails episodes requiring a timely useful contribution.

**Verify:** Run an all-silent fixture arm and inspect missed-event counts and gate failure.

### AC-2

Quietness from an observer gap is not scored as a correct no-change decision.

**Verify:** Replay a disconnected interval containing a labeled important event and inspect coverage versus attention errors.

### AC-3

Every reported improvement is paired to the same eligible baseline episode and evidence.

**Verify:** Reconcile arm manifests, excluded cases, labels, counts and uncertainty inputs.
