## Purpose

Measure whether Matter improves whole episodes while making overhead and uncertainty visible. Metrics must distinguish eligible, delivered, consumed, acted-upon and verified outcomes and must include important cases that stayed quiet.

## In scope

- Stable metric definitions, reason codes and bounded tracing.
- Episode-level reports, quiet-case sampling and actual cost/latency accounting.

## Scope boundaries

- Deploying or modifying a downstream project as part of this planning ticket without that implementation task.
- Treating synthetic checks as evidence of live provider or user outcomes.

## Implementation

1. Define event counters and denominators with scope, rule-family and model/config versions.
2. Aggregate outcomes by independent episode/source groups without treating duplicate reports as independent samples.
3. Expose compact audit reports that retain missing evidence and unknown consumption rather than requiring manual acknowledgments.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Delivery and verified benefit cannot be conflated in metrics or reports.

**Verify:** Replay an episode with delivered-but-unconsumed and consumed-without-verification updates and inspect separate counters.

### AC-2

Suppression cannot appear successful solely because nothing was emitted.

**Verify:** Include labeled important quiet cases and verify missed/late outcomes appear in the report denominator.

### AC-3

Reported overhead includes all evaluation, retrieval, retries and delivery blocking work.

**Verify:** Compare instrumented event totals with an independently measured episode trace and account for failed calls.
