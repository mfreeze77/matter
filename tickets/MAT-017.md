## Purpose

Implement domain projections separate from identity and attention. Ordinary recovery and reopening follow qualifying evidence automatically, with human decisions required only where the host assigns that authority.

## In scope

- Versioned edges and receipts
- Matching resolution and reopening triggers
- Correction and effective-time handling

## Scope boundaries

- Built-in civic or agent rules
- Mandatory closeout workflow

## Implementation

1. Validate profile edge, scope, current assessment, matching evidence, and authority.
2. Commit transition plus downstream invalidation atomically against revisions and epoch.
3. Handle late-discovered resolution with separate effective and knowledge time.
4. Reopen on declared recurrence or invalid resolution while retaining old transition history.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Only corresponding qualifying evidence resolves a condition.

**Verify:** Compare unrelated and matching success for two generic condition keys.

### AC-2

New occurrence can reopen; old-event redelivery cannot.

**Verify:** Resolve, replay the original event, then add a distinct happening.

### AC-3

Retraction weakens support without proving the opposite.

**Verify:** Retract sole resolution evidence and inspect profile-defined unknown/reopened state.
