## Purpose

Preserve useful cited research even when the personal assistant decides not to interrupt. Give later JEV stages authorized context about that user/client/angle without converting prior model interpretations into source facts, independent corroboration, delivered knowledge or qualified judgments.

## In scope

- Minimal review-finding and quiet/revisit history using existing records, receipts, dependencies and host storage ownership.
- Current-context selection of derived notes for later bounded JEV packets, including original evidence and uncertainty.
- Targeted invalidation and unchanged-loop suppression without discarding genuine corrections or deadlines.

## Scope boundaries

- Implementation, provider/tool execution, client-data processing or deployment as part of this planning commit.
- New assistant/model client, MCP server, tool wizard, source index, database, queue or general orchestration framework where existing owners supply the capability.
- Changing frozen monitoring 1.0/1.1 semantics silently; production alerts, formal qualification, DIAT or unrelated Oil work.

## Implementation

1. Map review findings to existing host records/Matter receipts rather than creating a new memory database or global client dossier. Separate exact source quotations/observations from assistant interpretations and suggested interests.
2. Bind each finding and review outcome to user/workspace/client-or-personal, approved objective revision and angle meaning, matter, source/citation revisions, availability, original method/attempt and actual access provenance. Angle identifier/granularity remains an open contract choice.
3. Retain why no alert was warranted and an optional reconsideration condition. Mark never communicated, report prepared, capture outcome unknown, actually conveyed, read and acted as separate states. An internal capture is not human delivery.
4. Include original citations and explicitly labelled derived context in subsequent authorized question packets. Do not count the note and its underlying source as separate corroboration; do not let the next model treat an earlier assumption as an established claim.
5. Recheck grants and original input/context bindings on reads/reuse. Source corrections, changed approved purpose/angle, pause or revocation invalidate or withhold pending results; shared source-only reads require each recipient access while private context stays isolated.
6. Suppress repeat requests for the unchanged gap/material/context using existing receipts and bounded investigation. New evidence, real corrections or deadlines can re-open review. Maintain replay availability order and original uncertainty.
7. Retain the distinct cited contributions and contextual rationale behind accumulated review pressure. Reprints, exact duplicates, correlated copies and repeated model summaries must not inflate it as independent evidence. A later fact may change the interpretation of earlier quiet evidence. Preserve current source/context invalidation; completed review clears reviewed pressure while evidence remains attached; decay before review and the exact reviewed-set boundary remain open implementation details.
8. Keep every scoped evidence/finding association, including quiet and contradictory research, after pressure reset. Record the completed review, alert/no-alert decision, actually reviewed source versions and ongoing monitoring-rule revisions/rationale. Retained or reviewed evidence is not automatically communicated, read, handled or resolved.
9. Reset only contributions covered by the completed review and current user/client/objective/angle. New or unreviewed material arriving during investigation, other client angles and later corrections remain eligible for their own pressure/review. Exact reset recovery is idempotent; failed/unknown/stale work cannot erase pending state or justify a blind retry.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Quiet review retains usable scoped context without advancing human communication.

**Verify:** Exercise a quiet early mention with citations, then a later material development; inspect retained findings, distinct receipt states and a justified new review without an invented prior send.

### AC-2

Derived findings cannot leak or become self-corroborating evidence.

**Verify:** Use two clients and two angles over one source; deny foreign-context reads, preserve original source/method labels, and verify repeated summary chains do not inflate independent evidence or create supported claims/certificates.

### AC-3

Reused findings remain current and bounded.

**Verify:** Verify edited objectives, source corrections, revoked grants, replay cuts, exact retries and unknown outcomes. An unchanged recheck does not produce another tool loop; a new deadline/correction is not suppressed by stale quiet feedback.

### AC-4

Accumulation reflects scoped distinct evidence rather than duplicate volume.

**Verify:** Replay weak distinct observations, repeated copies and derived summaries, then a material new event under two client angles. Preserve original citations and uncertainty, prevent duplicate inflation and cross-angle pressure leakage, and record which new evidence changed the review rationale. No decay/reset formula is invented.

### AC-5

Pressure reset never discards scoped evidence, unreviewed arrivals or monitoring-rule provenance.

**Verify:** For both alert and no-alert, assert reviewed pressure clears and all original/newly gathered source and finding associations remain inspectable under current access. Inject a concurrent unreviewed arrival and another client angle, verify neither is cleared, and preserve old/new authorized rule versions, unknown outcomes and communication receipts separately.
