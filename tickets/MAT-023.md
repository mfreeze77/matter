## Purpose

Provide read operations applications can render without rebuilding matter semantics in the UI. Explanations should distinguish current understanding, historical knowledge, unresolved evidence, and actual delivery outcomes.

## In scope

- Current/history projections and scoped pagination
- Why and missing-evidence queries
- Receipt reconstruction and unavailability

## Scope boundaries

- Dashboard product or essay generator

## Implementation

1. Define consistent scoped queries with as-of boundaries, redirects, and incomplete flags.
2. Compose explanations from actual rule decisions and operation receipts, retaining citations and disagreement.
3. Resolve immutable references once and expose unavailable retained material.
4. Prevent projections from combining incompatible identity, assessment, and delivery revisions during concurrent changes.
