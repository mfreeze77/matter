## Purpose

Make qualification an executable admission boundary rather than a report attached to a threshold. Even a well-formed confident answer remains unqualified when its model, semantic definition, evidence preparation, domain or validity period does not match the reviewed evaluation record.

## In scope

- Certificate schema, applicability matching, expiry/revocation and decisions
- Separate affirmative/negative thresholds and abstention

## Scope boundaries

- Declaring any model family universally safe
- Automatic authority or speaker identity assignments

## Implementation

1. Define certificate identity over resolved model, question/rubric/options, renderer, preparation profile, eligible domain, measure, thresholds, support and evaluation artifacts.
2. Validate applicability before execution where possible and again before admission, retaining explicit missing, failed, expired, revoked and mismatched reasons.
3. Allow raw shadow observations without an applicable certificate while preventing them from producing consequential qualified judgments.
4. Evaluate positive and negative proposition decisions with separately declared regions and preserve disagreement or insufficient evidence.
5. Invalidate dependent current assessments when a certificate is revoked while keeping historical receipts and their then-applicable status.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

A stored threshold from a failed held-out record cannot qualify an answer above that threshold.

**Verify:** Use a Jevcal-shaped failed record with a confident fixture answer and assert rejection.

### AC-2

Every identity-bearing mismatch and expiry produces a specific admission reason.

**Verify:** Run a certificate applicability matrix across model, wording, options, profile, scope and time.

### AC-3

Revocation removes current eligibility without rewriting historical evaluation evidence.

**Verify:** Admit, revoke, replay history and inspect both states and dependent invalidation.
