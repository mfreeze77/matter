## Purpose

Build the relationship layer that lets a source support one claim while qualifying another. Preserve wording and locators, and prevent ingested assertions from changing established state without an assessment and authorized transition.

## In scope

- Versioned propositions and qualifiers
- Cited evidence relation kinds
- Locator validation and missing-source reasons

## Scope boundaries

- Domain truth rules
- Automatic transitions or identity assignments

## Implementation

1. Define claim subjects, predicates, values, attribution, qualifiers, and temporal scope.
2. Implement multiple accepted relations targeting whole propositions or named components.
3. Accept explicit adapter locator-validation receipts, distinguishing exact passages from whole artifacts.
4. Append claim corrections and emit dependency changes without rewriting source wording.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Support and qualification can coexist for one passage without losing scope.

**Verify:** Inspect mixed-relation fixtures and proposition components.

### AC-2

Ingesting an assertion does not change matter state or merge identity.

**Verify:** Submit asserted approvals and equivalence claims; verify only claims appear.

### AC-3

Unavailable or invalid citations are explicit.

**Verify:** Exercise missing bytes, absent quotation, reversed lines, and declared whole-artifact citations.
