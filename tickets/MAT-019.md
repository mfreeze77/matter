## Purpose

Build the gate that turns a long history into a useful update. Repeated internal changes should not become repeated interruptions, and quietness must distinguish no material change from inadequate evidence.

## In scope

- Attention decisions and neutral treatments
- Audience-purpose material deltas
- Quiet reasons and decision-request criteria

## Scope boundaries

- Transport implementations
- Universal importance score

## Implementation

1. Compare the current assessment with the audience-purpose delivered baseline.
2. Apply material change, relevance, ownership, dispositions, and decision-need rules.
3. Return quiet, host_hint, digest, update, correction, or decision_request with reasons and dependencies.
4. Preserve insufficient evidence and incomplete coverage without automatically escalating to the human.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

Equivalent reassessments do not create another update for an informed audience.

**Verify:** Deliver a baseline, add paraphrases, and inspect reasons.

### AC-2

A decisive correction can become eligible without a new positive event.

**Verify:** Invalidate a previously delivered conclusion and evaluate its correction delta.

### AC-3

Low confidence alone does not generate a human decision request.

**Verify:** Use ambiguous evidence without a decision reserved for that person.
