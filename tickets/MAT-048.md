## Purpose

Consume copied crawler packages as observed source claims and occurrences, link them to continuing matters through scoped identifiers, and preserve civic stages as qualified claims. No parsed statement becomes accepted legal state merely because it is imported.

## In scope

- Read-only copied-package adapter
- Stable matter/occurrence/instrument references
- Claim-relative evidence and stage dimensions
- Ambiguous associations, corrections and negative coverage

## Scope boundaries

- Mutating existing receiver identities or matter tables
- Title-only/case-number-only merges
- Inferring approval, applicant identity or funding from topic overlap

## Implementation

1. Map existing source claims, agenda/item/motion IDs, raw case/instrument numbers and source spans to common observations and occurrences; retain issuer, jurisdiction, type and year scope.
2. Attach claims through explicit typed relationships; represent two reports of the same meeting as separate observations of one occurrence and a later hearing as a new occurrence.
3. Preserve motion intent, stated vote result, mechanics and body authority as independent inputs; calculate nothing when the required operands/rules are unknown.
4. Represent discussion, recommendation, funding, procurement, award, effective date and cancellation as independently supported domain predicates, including prerequisites and uncertainty.
5. Hash and checkpoint imported packages, record all unsupported/missing fields and propagate source withdrawal/correction into the existing dependency mechanism without rewriting original observations.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

A repeated package creates no new occurrence or assessment while a new hearing of the same case does.

**Verify:** Replay duplicated and later-meeting fixtures and assert stable matters, distinct occurrence counts and source links.

### AC-2

Identifier collisions and distinct instruments remain unresolved or separate.

**Verify:** Use same-number different-year/jurisdiction fixtures and one case with multiple ordinances; verify no unscoped merge.

### AC-3

Failed approval and recommendation cannot become final adopted state.

**Verify:** Run claim/stage fixtures with known/unknown authority and thresholds and inspect all proposed assessment predicates.

### AC-4

Source corrections preserve the old observation and invalidate only dependent current views.

**Verify:** Replace one claim source revision, then compare historical/as-of and current assessments plus pending delivery eligibility.
